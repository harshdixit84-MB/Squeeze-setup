The idea: buy the next morning after the 20-day average (EMA) crosses above the 50-day average on a green day. Sell at +8% (a "hit"), or the next morning after the price closes below the 50-day average (the stoploss). These are past results and promise nothing. The list is today's mid/small-cap list, so stocks that dropped out of it are missing, which makes results look better than real life. Cost assumed: 0.2% per trade. "Days" are calendar days.
