20/50 EMA Crossover - monthly

Loading...

Rule (monthly chart): the 20-month average (EMA) closed above the 50-month average in the last fully closed month, after being at or below it the month before, and that month's candle was green (close higher than open). The month that is still running is not counted. Stocks come from the Squeeze tab of your Google Sheet. New ones are added in the first scan after a month ends and stay here until you delete them. Prices, "since crossover" and the latest-day change refresh every trading day. Click a stock name to open its chart in TradingView.

VTD 4-hour BUY signals

Loading...

Rule (Viru Trend Dynamics, default settings, long side): on a confirmed 4-hour candle close the trend line is rising (slope above 0.08 x ATR) and the close is above the upper band, while no earlier VTD trade is still open. Each BUY is tracked as a virtual trade: entry = that close, stop-loss from the last 12 candles (0.75 - 3 x ATR away), target 1:3, and an ATR trailing stop after +2R. Stocks come from the Squeeze tab of your Google Sheet. The list is refreshed twice a day (after the 13:15 and 15:30 candles) and stocks stay until you delete them. Click a stock to open its 4-hour chart in TradingView.

Monthly Supertrend

Loading...

Rule: on the monthly chart the Supertrend (10, 3) changed colour on the last closed month. Green list: it turned green and that month's candle is not a hammer and has no long upper wick. Red list: the mirror image (not a shooting star, no long lower wick). Stocks come from the Squeeze tab of your Google Sheet, prices from Angel One. SP pullback: stocks from the green / red lists whose trend is still the same colour and whose price has come back to within 3% of the Supertrend line (green: price above the line; red: price below it). They leave this view when the price moves more than 3% away. Stocks stay in the green / red lists until you delete them or tick Buy. Click a stock name to open its monthly chart in TradingView.

Squeeze List

Loading...

Every stock in the Squeeze tab of your Google Sheet. Click a stock name to open its chart in TradingView. The list refreshes every morning and with each Daily scan. Price is the last close from Angel One (updated after the market closes, not a live price).

Calculate (weekly or monthly backtest, last 10 years, one stock at a time, prices from Angel One): the 20 EMA closes above the 50 EMA on a closed weekly (or monthly) candle, the trade starts at the next week's (month's) open and ends at +30% (target) or -20% from entry (stop), whichever the daily candles show first. After a +30% exit it also tells whether price later reached +70% (trail target hit) or fell to -20% first (stoploss hit). The next trade needs a fresh crossover after the previous trade has closed. If target and stop fall inside one daily candle, the stop is assumed first.
